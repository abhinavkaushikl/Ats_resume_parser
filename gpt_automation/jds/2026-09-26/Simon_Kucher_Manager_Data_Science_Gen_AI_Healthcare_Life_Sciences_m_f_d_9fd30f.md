How You Will Create An Impact

- You will help leading pharmaceutical, biotech, medtech, and healthcare organizations unlock value from data by designing and implementing Advanced Analytics, AI, and Generative AI solutions.

- You will extract, process, and analyze complex structured and unstructured datasets across the Life Sciences value chain, including commercial, medical, patient, market, and R&D data.

- You will leverage your technical expertise to develop, test, and deploy Machine Learning and Generative AI solutions that address critical business challenges and support data-driven decision making.

- You will combine Advanced Analytics, Machine Learning, and Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate actionable insights, automate knowledge-intensive processes, and accelerate business outcomes for Life Sciences clients.

- You will translate business questions into analytical approaches and AI use cases, ensuring that results are robust, scalable, and aligned with real-world impact.

- You will take ownership of workstreams, collaborate closely with client stakeholders, and communicate technical findings in a clear and business-relevant way.

- You will present analyses, AI solutions, and strategic recommendations to client teams and senior executives.

- You will contribute to the development of innovative offerings at the intersection of Data Science, Generative AI, and Life Sciences.

About You

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Economics, Bioinformatics, Life Sciences, or a related quantitative field.

- Fluency in German and English, with strong written and verbal communication skills in both languages.

- 4–6 years of experience in Data Science, Machine Learning, AI, or Advanced Analytics.

- Strong experience developing, deploying, and scaling Machine Learning models using Python or similar technologies.

- Solid understanding of AI and Machine Learning methodologies, including tree-based methods, Bayesian approaches, and modern Generative AI technologies.

- Hands-on experience applying Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, or related GenAI frameworks to solve business challenges.

- Familiarity with cloud-based data and AI platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP) and their services for data storage, compute, automation, Machine Learning, and application deployment.

- Knowledge of relational, SQL, and NoSQL databases, as well as Data Engineering and ETL/ELT processes.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex data into actionable business insights.

- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.

- Proactive, entrepreneurial, and collaborative mindset, with a passion for leveraging data and AI to solve complex business challenges.

- Experience in consulting is a strong plus.

- Experience in pharma, medtech or consumer health is a strong plus, including familiarity with industry data, business processes, and challenges across pricing, market access and commercial functions.
