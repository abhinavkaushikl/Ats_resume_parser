# Project prep - Retail Demand Forecasting Pipeline

Added for: Senior Applied Scientist - Demand Forecast at Zalando (Berlin)
Apply: https://jobs.zalando.com/en/jobs/2725145
Company profile: Zalando is a European e-commerce platform connecting customers with fashion and lifestyle brands, and builds forecasting tools that help partner brands plan demand.

## On the resume

- Built a hierarchical demand forecasting pipeline for retail product-level sales data, combining XGBoost and LSTM models to capture cross-product and temporal patterns.
- Evaluated forecast accuracy against statistical baseline methods and served predictions through a FastAPI service with MLflow for model tracking and versioning.

**Technologies:** Python, SQL, PySpark, XGBoost, LSTM, FastAPI, MLflow, Pandas
