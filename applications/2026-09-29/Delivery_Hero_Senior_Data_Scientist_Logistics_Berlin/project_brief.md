# Project prep - Courier ETA Prediction Engine

Added for: Senior Data Scientist at Delivery Hero (Berlin)
Company profile: Delivery Hero is a Berlin-headquartered local delivery platform operating in around 65 countries, connecting customers with restaurants, grocery and retail through its courier network.

## On the resume

- Built an ETA prediction model for on-demand last-mile logistics, combining order, distance and courier-supply features in LightGBM and XGBoost regression with quantile estimates of late-arrival risk.
- Evaluated it with time-based backtesting against historical baselines, tracked experiments in MLflow, and automated retraining and batch prediction with PySpark and Airflow on BigQuery data.

**Technologies:** BigQuery, Spark, Python, scikit-learn, LightGBM, XGBoost, MLflow, Airflow
