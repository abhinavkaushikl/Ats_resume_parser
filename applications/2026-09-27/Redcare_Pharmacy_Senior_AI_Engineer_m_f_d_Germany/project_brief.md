# Project prep - Medication Information Assistant

Added for: Senior AI Engineer at Redcare Pharmacy (Germany)
Apply: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/Redcare-Pharmacy/744000149802610
Company profile: Redcare Pharmacy is a European online pharmacy selling prescription and over-the-counter medicines and health products to consumers across several countries.

## On the resume

- Built a RAG assistant for online pharmacy customer questions on medicine use and interactions, grounding Claude answers in product leaflets with LangGraph and guardrails that escalate uncertain cases.
- Evaluated answers with LLM evaluators against pharmacist-reviewed question sets, versioned prompts and runs in MLflow, and served it through FastAPI in Docker with tracing.

**Technologies:** Python, LangGraph, Claude, RAG, pgvector, MLflow, FastAPI, Docker
