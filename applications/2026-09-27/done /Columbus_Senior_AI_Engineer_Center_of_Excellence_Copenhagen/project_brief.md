## On the resume

- Built a multi-agent assistant that answers employee questions over enterprise documents, combining hybrid search, embeddings and cross-encoder reranking with citation grounding so every answer points to its source.
- Evaluated faithfulness and citation accuracy with LLM evaluators and tracing, then served the agents through FastAPI on Azure with access-aware retrieval and audit logging.

**Technologies:** Azure OpenAI, LangGraph, Vector Search, Embeddings, Cross-Encoder, FastAPI, Docker, LLM Evals
