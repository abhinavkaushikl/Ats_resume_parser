## Job description

**Our Story**Unum Technology Centre in Carlow serves as a strategic software development and IT services centre supporting Unum, a leading provider of income protection in the US. Our team of IT professionals build solutions and critical business applications to digitally transform the way we do business.

We are seeking a highly skilled Senior AI Engineer to help shape and deliver the next generation of AI-powered solutions across our organization. This is an exciting opportunity to work with cutting-edge technologies including Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Machine Learning, MLOps, and AWS cloud services, building scalable, secure, and production-ready AI solutions that drive meaningful business impact.

As a senior member of the team, you will play a key role in designing AI architectures, developing intelligent applications, and establishing best practices that advance our AI capabilities across the enterprise.

**The Role**

* Design, develop, and deploy enterprise-scale AI and Machine Learning solutions, including Generative AI and LLM-powered applications.
* Build AI solutions using modern architectures such as RAG, AI Agents, vector search, and enterprise knowledge assistants.
* Develop and optimize AI applications on AWS using services such as Bedrock, SageMaker, Lambda, ECS/EKS, and S3.
* Lead the implementation of robust MLOps and LLMOps practices, including model deployment, monitoring, versioning, and lifecycle management.
* Design and develop APIs, integrations, and cloud-native services that enable AI capabilities across the business.
* Ensure AI solutions are scalable, secure, reliable, and aligned with responsible AI and governance standards.
* Collaborate with business stakeholders, data scientists, and engineering teams while mentoring colleagues and promoting engineering best practice

**What You Offer**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, Mathematics, or a related discipline.
* 7+ years of experience in Software Engineering, Machine Learning Engineering, AI Engineering, or a related technical field.
* Proven experience delivering production AI and Machine Learning solutions in cloud environments.
* Strong Python development skills and experience with modern AI/ML frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, LangChain, or similar technologies.
* Hands-on experience building Generative AI solutions, including LLMs, RAG architectures, AI Agents, or vector search technologies.
* Experience designing and supporting MLOps/LLMOps pipelines, CI/CD automation, and model lifecycle management.
* Strong AWS experience, including services such as SageMaker, Bedrock, Lambda, ECS/EKS, and S3.
* Experience with modern software engineering practices, including APIs, microservices, Docker, Kubernetes, and Infrastructure-as-Code technologies such as Terraform or CloudFormation.
