# Project prep - Insurance Claims Document Assistant

Added for: Senior AI Engineer at Unum Ireland (Carlow)
Apply: https://ie.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-ai-engineer-at-unum-ireland-4460116229
Company profile: Unum Ireland runs the Unum Technology Centre in Carlow, building software and AI solutions for Unum, a US provider of income protection insurance.

## On the resume

- Built a RAG and agent-based assistant for insurance claim and policy documents, extracting fields with Amazon Textract and answering questions over a vector index through Claude on AWS Bedrock.
- Evaluated answers with LLM evaluators and tracing, versioned prompts and models in MLflow, and deployed the FastAPI service in Docker on Amazon ECS through Terraform and CI/CD.

**Technologies:** AWS Bedrock, Amazon Textract, Amazon ECS, LangChain, pgvector, MLflow, FastAPI, Terraform
