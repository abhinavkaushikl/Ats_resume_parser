# Project prep - Store Demand Forecasting Engine

Added for: Staff Data Scientist, Dmarts - Quick Commerce at Delivery Hero (Berlin)
Apply: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/DeliveryHero/744000137801469-staff-data-scientist-dmarts-quick-commerce
Company profile: Delivery Hero is a Berlin-based local delivery platform whose quick commerce stores deliver groceries and everyday products in minutes.

## On the resume

- Built a store and product-level demand forecasting engine for quick-commerce replenishment, combining Holt-Winters baselines with LightGBM and LSTM models on promotion, seasonality and stock-out features.
- Evaluated forecasts with rolling backtests against statistical baselines, scheduled retraining in Airflow with MLflow tracking, and served forecasts to planners through FastAPI in Docker.

**Technologies:** Python, LightGBM, LSTM, Airflow, MLflow, Docker, GCP, PySpark
