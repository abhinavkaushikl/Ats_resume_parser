# Project prep - Claim Frequency and Severity Models

## On the resume

- Built claim frequency and severity models for motor insurance pricing, combining LightGBM with Poisson and Gamma objectives and adding text embeddings of claim notes as extra features.
- Evaluated the models with time-based validation, lift charts and SHAP explanations, then tracked experiments in MLflow and deployed scoring pipelines on Databricks with Apache Spark.

**Technologies:** Python, Azure ML, MLflow, Databricks, Apache Spark, LightGBM, SQL, Parquet
