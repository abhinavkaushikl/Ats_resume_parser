Apply: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/Sportradar/744000150313500

## On the resume

- Built pre-match and in-play probability models for football outcomes, combining a Poisson GLM with Bayesian team-strength priors and time-series features of recent form.
- Evaluated calibration and log loss on held-out seasons against market odds, and packaged the models as a tested library with documented versions for downstream pricing.

**Technologies:** Python, GLM, Bayesian Statistics, PyMC, XGBoost, Pandas, SQL, Git
