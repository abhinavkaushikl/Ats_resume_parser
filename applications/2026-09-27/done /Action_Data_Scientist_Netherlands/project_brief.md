# Project prep - Store Demand Forecasting Engine

Added for: Data Scientist at Action (Zwaagdijk-Oost)
Apply: https://nl.action.jobs/en/jobs/data-scientist-e110469
Company profile: Action is a European non-food discount retailer with stores across Europe, whose central Data Analytics team builds models for supply chain, commerce, store operations and real estate.

## On the resume

- Built a store and product-level demand forecasting engine for discount retail, combining Holt-Winters baselines with LightGBM on PySpark features to support replenishment planning for supply chain teams.
- Evaluated forecasts with rolling backtests against the baseline, tracked runs in MLflow on Databricks, and served forecasts through a Dockerised FastAPI service with drift and accuracy monitoring.

**Technologies:** Databricks, MLflow, PySpark, Python, SQL, Azure, LightGBM, Docker
