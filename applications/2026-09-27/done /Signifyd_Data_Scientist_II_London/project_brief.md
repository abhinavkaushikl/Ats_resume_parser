# Project prep - E-commerce Fraud Risk Scoring

Added for: Data Scientist II at Signifyd (London)
Apply: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/signifyd95/jobs/8103276
Company profile: Signifyd provides fraud protection and risk decisioning for e-commerce merchants, using production ML models to approve good orders and block fraudulent ones.

## On the resume

- Built a fraud risk model for online transactions with LightGBM on behavioural and order features engineered in PySpark, tuning decision thresholds to balance approvals against fraud losses.
- Evaluated decisioning strategies through offline backtests and a simulated A/B test on holdout orders, tracked experiments in MLflow, and served scores through a FastAPI service.

**Technologies:** Python, SQL, Spark, Databricks, LightGBM, MLflow, FastAPI, GCP
