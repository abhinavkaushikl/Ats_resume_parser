# Project prep - Merchant Recommendation Ranking System

Added for: Senior Data Scientist, AI Engineering at Mastercard (Dublin)
Apply: https://ie.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-data-scientist-ai-engineering-at-mastercard-4468990890
Company profile: Mastercard runs a global payments network, and its AI Centre of Excellence builds machine learning and foundation-model capabilities on large-scale transaction data.

## On the resume

- Built a merchant recommendation system for cardholders, generating candidates from transaction embeddings learned in PyTorch and re-ranking them with a LightGBM ranker trained on past purchase behaviour.
- Evaluated rankings offline with NDCG and holdout experiments against a popularity baseline, tracked experiments in MLflow and served batch recommendations from Databricks with Spark.

**Technologies:** Python, LightGBM, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Databricks, Spark, MLflow, SQL
