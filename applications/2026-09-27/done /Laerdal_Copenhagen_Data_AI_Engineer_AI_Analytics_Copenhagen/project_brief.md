Added for: Data AI Engineer (AI & Analytics) at Laerdal Copenhagen (Copenhagen)

## On the resume

- Built an agentic data analyst for training outcome data in LangChain, answering educator questions by writing SQL over a governed data model and combining results with statistical summaries.
- Evaluated answers against analyst-written test questions with LLM evaluators and tracing, added guardrails on data access, and served it on Databricks with PySpark and FastAPI.

**Technologies:** LangChain, LangGraph, Databricks, PySpark, SQL, RAG, FastAPI, Python
