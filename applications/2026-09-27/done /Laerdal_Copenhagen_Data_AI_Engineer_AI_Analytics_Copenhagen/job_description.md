## Job description

*Building trusted AI-powered analytics for healthcare training outcomes*

**Make an impact with Laerdal!**

**Laerdal Medical** is a world leader in healthcare education and resuscitation training that uses immersive technologies and data-centric insights to increase survival and improve healthcare quality. **Laerdal Copenhagen** is our Center of excellence for Digital Learning, and on a global scale, we work with leading life-saving institutions around the world including emergency services, hospitals, ambulance services, first aid organizations, and educational institutions. Our products help these organizations save lives every day. We are a dynamic and energetic organization consisting of people from a wide variety of cultures and backgrounds. Our goal is to help save one million more lives, every year, by 2030, and to do so sustainably.

**Position overview**

We work in the space of advanced analytics for learners in healthcare and are looking to expand our team. In our area of Resuscitation Quality Improvement (RQI) we serve as the interface for varying roles in the healthcare system that want to know how their learners are doing, where they can improve and how prioritizing a high-quality training curriculum elevates the skills of their workforce.

Our product team spans multiple roles, backgrounds and nationalities and for the next major phase in how we deliver insights to users, we want your help:

Do you understand how to translate a vast structured data foundation into actionable insights for different user groups and metrics?

Do you have experience in advanced analytics, i.e. leveraging LLMs, statistics and ML? Have you implemented agentic systems? Do you know how to move from product idea to trustworthy user-facing feature in this space?

Do you want to help us on our mission to help save (even more) lives?

If you answered YES to those questions, don’t hesitate to apply with a brief personal motivation and CV and we might soon be getting to know each other in person!

**What we want to build with you:**

* **AI-Ready data foundation:** guaranteeing high data quality for AI, refining the data model and ensuring efficient retrieval/search across our domain
* **Agentic data analyst:** empower our analytics users to uncover individualized insights by combining traditional analytics with agentic workloads.
* **Operational excellence for AI:** continuously learning and adapting the system while collaborating closely with product and design experts on the team

**How you will contribute to the team:**

* Design and implement **agentic product features** for our custom advanced analytics platform
* Understand and improve our domain data model
* Partner with the **Product Manager** and **Design Researcher** to understand user needs, gather requirements and translate them into technical solutions
* Ensure security/compliance and governed access for customers and internal users as well as safe guardrails for AI

**Which skills you should bring (AI & data):**

* Hands-on experience in data modeling, **production ready LLM** solutions and using tools such as Langchain, Mastra, Embabel or other **ADKs**
* Proficiency in using cloud data platforms and corresponding tools; e.g. **PySpark**, SQL, **Databricks** as well as the tools that enable search indexes, **RAG** or other integration patterns
* Proven understanding of different **agent workflow patterns** and their benefits
* (optional) Experience in data governance, semantic models and metadata management

**Software engineering & other skills:**

* Knowledge of **engineering practices** and willingness to discuss them with peers; e.g. branching strategies, TDD, agile & DevOps principles
* Advanced abilities in AI coding: **harness engineering**, skills development, context engineering, etc.
* (optional) Knowledge in IaC tools and cloud solution architecture
* (optional) Domain knowledge in healthcare and/or learning systems

Join a mission-driven team where your work helps improve healthcare and save lives around the world. You'll enjoy a high-trust, low-bureaucracy environment that values autonomy, collaboration, craftsmanship, and continuous learning.
