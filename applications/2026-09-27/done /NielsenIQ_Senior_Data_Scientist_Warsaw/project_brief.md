# Project prep - Retail Sales Trend Estimation

Added for: Senior Data Scientist at NielsenIQ (Warsaw)
Apply: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/NielsenIQ/744000149266456
Company profile: NielsenIQ is a consumer intelligence company that measures retail sales and consumer buying behaviour for brands and retailers in 100+ markets.

## On the resume

- Built a trend-estimation pipeline for retail sales panels with incomplete store coverage, reweighting samples and imputing missing outlets with gradient boosting in PySpark and Pandas.
- Validated estimates against held-out stores with unit-tested checks and bias diagnostics, then packaged the pipeline as a command-line tool in Docker for scheduled runs on Azure.

**Technologies:** Python, Apache Spark, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, LightGBM, Docker, Azure
