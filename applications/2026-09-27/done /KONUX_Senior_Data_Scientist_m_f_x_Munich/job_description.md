# Senior Data Scientist (m/f/x)

Munich

**About Us:**

KONUX is a dynamic and fast-growing technology company that is transforming the way the railway industry works. Our AI-powered solutions empower our customers to make smarter, data-driven decisions, and our culture is fueled by grit, ingenuity, and credibility - we believe that these values are essential to unlocking the potential of technology and making a real impact. At KONUX, we are passionate about driving innovation in the fields of IoT and AI, and we are looking for like-minded individuals to join our team.

**Job Description:**

We are seeking a **Senior** **Data Scientist (f/m/x)** to join our team of innovators. In this role, you will be at the forefront of developing groundbreaking solutions that solve real-world problems. You will work alongside a team of experts who are committed to pushing the boundaries of what's possible and driving innovation forward. If you're a go-getter who's not afraid to think outside the box, we want to hear from you. You'll be a key driver of our culture, helping to ensure that our values are embedded in everything we do.

**Your Impact:**

* Develop insights using a wide range of data sources, including IIoT sensor data and other railway domain datasets
* Design and implement solutions - from understanding customer challenges to releasing models to production
* Deploy well-tested, maintainable solutions into live systems
* Develop tools and infrastructure for sensor data analysis that integrate smoothly into our end-to-end software engineering lifecycle
* Collaborate closely with engineers, product managers, and domain experts to translate business needs into technical impact\
* Drive innovation and help you and your colleagues improve and grow

**To be successful in this role, you’ll need to have:**

* A degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field with **5+ years** of hands-on experience in data science, having **built and tested** statistical or ML models
* **Fluency in Python** and ideally experience with an additional programming language
* Past experience working with dataframe-centric **data manipulation tools** such as pandas, PySpark, Polars and SQL
* Past experience **owning complex data science problems** independently, having developed **models for production systems**, beyond pure academic or research settings
* Familiarity with **version control (Git)** and common **software engineering best-practices**
* A **proactive, impact-driven attitude** and the ability to **thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment**

##

**It’s a plus (but not a requirement) if you have:**

## **Experience applying digital signal processing (DSP) techniques to extract features and insights, for example from sensor or time-series data**

* Understanding of **model monitoring techniques**, **software testing**, and **CI/CD workflows**
* Worked with and trained models using **diverse types of data** (e.g. time-series, tabular, event logs, images, sensor data etc.)
* Experience with **state-space and survival modeling**, and familiarity with statistical packages such as statsmodels, PyMC, or Stan
* Experience with **AWS** and **Databricks**
