# Project prep - Enterprise Metadata Knowledge Assistant

Added for: Senior Data Scientist at ABN AMRO Bank N.V. (Netherlands)
Apply: https://nl.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-data-scientist-at-abn-amro-bank-n-v-4472086565
Company profile: ABN AMRO is a Dutch bank whose Enterprise Enabling Data Domain builds AI and business insight products for teams across the bank.

## On the resume

- Built an agentic RAG assistant in LangGraph that answered questions over enterprise data catalogues and metadata, combining vector retrieval with a knowledge graph of dataset lineage and ownership.
- Evaluated answers with LLM evaluators on a labelled question set, tracked runs in MLflow on Databricks, and served the assistant through FastAPI with tracing to support model governance reviews.

**Technologies:** Azure, Databricks, Spark, Python, LangGraph, pgvector, ArangoDB, MLflow
