# Project prep - Manufacturing Quality Deviation Predictor

Added for: Senior Data Scientist at Abbott (Sligo)
Apply: https://www.jobs.abbott/us/en/job/31158582/Senior-Data-Scientist
Company profile: Abbott is a global healthcare company; its Diabetes Care site in Sligo makes diabetes technology and is building a Digital, Data & AI team.

## On the resume

- Built a LightGBM model that flagged likely batch quality deviations in medical device manufacturing from process and test data, with feature-level explanations for quality reviewers.
- Validated the model on held-out batches with documented assumptions and limitations, tracked versions in MLflow on Databricks, and shared risk scores with stakeholders in Power BI.

**Technologies:** Databricks, Python, SQL, Power BI, PySpark, LightGBM, MLflow
