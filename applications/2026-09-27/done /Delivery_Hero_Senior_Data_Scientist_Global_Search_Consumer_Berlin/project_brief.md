- Evaluated rankers offline with NDCG and MRR against a gradient-boosted baseline, tracked experiments in MLflow, and served the model through FastAPI with latency and drift monitoring.

**Technologies:** PyTorch, Python, PySpark, SQL, LightGBM, MLflow, FastAPI, Vector DB
