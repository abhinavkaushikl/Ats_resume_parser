# Project prep - Customer Behaviour Segmentation Models

Added for: Senior Applied AI Scientist at Intent HQ (London)
Apply: https://intenthq.teamtailor.com/jobs/8360453-senior-applied-ai-scientist
Company profile: Intent HQ builds behavioural AI that turns enterprises' first party customer data into context and insight for personalisation and decisions.

## On the resume

- Built behavioural segmentation for customer event data, embedding activity sequences with a transformer encoder and clustering them with DBSCAN to find groups that predict churn and engagement.
- Evaluated segments with holdout XGBoost propensity models and stakeholder reviews, then served them as features through Databricks and FastAPI for use in downstream client models.

**Technologies:** PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, PySpark, Spark SQL, Databricks, XGBoost, DBSCAN, FastAPI
