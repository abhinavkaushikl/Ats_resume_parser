**Your Tasks**

* Design and build data science solutions, across TÜV SÜD domains (e.g., medical technology, elevators, automotive, product testing, …).
* Engineer LLM solutions, in particular in the area of conversational AI, end to end: prompt design / refinement, retrieval pipelines, safety / guardrails, and systematic evaluation.
* Support in monitoring and improving deployed solutions by screening quality, latency, and cost; triaging user feedback; and resolving incidents.
* Collaborate in cross‑functional, agile teams with data scientists, software engineers, and subject‑matter experts.
* Prototype, experiment, and benchmark new approaches.
* Identify trends in unstructured data processing and sourcing (OCR / document parsing, embeddings, etc.)
* Contribute to internal tooling, documentation, and enablement for LLM development and evaluation.

**Your Qualification**

* Successfully completed a degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Physics, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field
* At least 4 years of practical experience applying data science methods in a corporate or consulting environment
* In-depth expertise in modern AI technologies, including large language models (LLMs), generative AI, and AI-powered business solutions
* Strong proficiency in Python for data science, combined with solid software engineering fundamentals
* Excellent communication skills and the ability to independently lead projects end-to-end, including effective collaboration with non-technical business stakeholders
* Experience with information retrieval techniques, especially retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), is a plus.
* Familiarity with Microsoft Azure and Azure DevOps is advantageous.
* Proficiency in German is highly desirable
