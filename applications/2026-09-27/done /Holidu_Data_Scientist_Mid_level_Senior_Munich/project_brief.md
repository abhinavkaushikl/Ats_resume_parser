## On the resume

- Built a booking demand forecasting and price recommendation model for vacation rentals, combining LightGBM on listing, seasonality and lead-time features with price elasticity estimates per segment.
- Validated forecasts with rolling time-based backtests and pricing through a simulated A/B test, then scheduled retraining in Airflow and tracked models and drift in MLflow.

**Technologies:** Python, SQL, Airflow, dbt, MLflow, LightGBM, XGBoost
