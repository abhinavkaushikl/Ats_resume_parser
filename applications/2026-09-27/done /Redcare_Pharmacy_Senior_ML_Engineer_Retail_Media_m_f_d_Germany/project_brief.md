# Project prep - Sponsored Product Ranking Model

Added for: Senior ML Engineer, Retail Media at Redcare Pharmacy (Germany)
Apply: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/Redcare-Pharmacy/744000150623869
Company profile: Redcare Pharmacy is a European online pharmacy selling prescription and over-the-counter medicines and health products to consumers across several countries.

## On the resume

- Built a sponsored product ranking model for online retail search, blending organic relevance with advertiser bids using LightGBM learning-to-rank on PySpark-engineered click and purchase features.
- Evaluated offline with time-based splits to avoid leakage, checked for position bias and distribution shift, and served ranking through FastAPI with MLflow-tracked models.

**Technologies:** Python, LightGBM, PySpark, SQL, MLflow, FastAPI, Docker, Airflow
