## Job description

We think the future is data science. It is the field which combines technology & domain knowledge – leading to amazing outcomes and even more amazing failures. Do you want to keep growing in this domain and are you as passionate as we are about data science (e.g. overexcited sharing of how Bayesian probability works during a birthday party?) – then join us in shaping the future of Agri-Tech.

Kramp Group discovers and builds e-commerce, supply chain, finance & data science solutions that are aiming at giving the ag-tech industry a push forward. We are building a digital platform, serving thousands of customers and generating a turnover of over one billion euros per year. You will join our centralized Data Science team – but will work dedicated for a product/business team.

**Your Role**

* Build data products using Cloud Build and Docker.
* Develop pipelines utilizing thousands of CPUs with Kubeflow.
* Develop endpoints using GPU’s to improve your domain.
* Specialize in one of the following domains with our ecommerce and supply chain operation : Recommenders, Search, Content, Operations and Supply Chain
* Translate business problems into data science solutions.
* Work at least 3 days per week physically from our office in Utrecht or Varsseveld, with the remaining days (optionally) remote.

**Why Join Us?**

* Innovative Environment: We use cutting-edge technologies & AI tools, while validating everything through experimentation.
* Empowerment: We believe in empowering individuals to take control of their own growth, with guidance provided.
* Transparent Culture: We offer a transparent environment with a mature management team that has a clear vision and specific goals.
* Work-Life Balance: We prioritize work-life balance and offer flexible working arrangements.

**Who are you?**

* A completed Master's degree, preferably in Data Science or a related field.
* Minimum 5 experience in Data Scientist is preferred; however, we encourage outstanding entry-level candidates to apply.
* Strong skills in SQL and Python (OOP).
* Experienced working in CI/CD environments & Git.
* Experience with Google Cloud, LightGBM and Kubeflow is beneficial.
* Excellent communication skills in English.

**What do we offer?**

At Kramp, we believe meaningful rewards help people grow, perform, and thrive. Our reward package is designed to be fair, transparent, and competitive, giving you confidence and support to do your best work.

We offer an attractive salary of €5,062 - €6,328 gross per month, depending on your experience and qualifications.

**The Compensation Package Also Includes**

* 25 vacation days and 12 ADV days (based on a fulltime contract)
* Flexible working policy in hours and location. Please note that this is a hybrid position, requiring a minimum of 2 days per week on-site at either our Utrecht office or Varsseveld headquarters (QH).
* Travel and working from home allowance
* Profit sharing bonus
* Great budget for training and courses
* Generous pension plan
