# Project prep - Spare Parts Demand Forecasting

Added for: Data Scientist at Kramp (Varsseveld)
Apply: https://nl.linkedin.com/jobs/view/data-scientist-at-kramp-4450738416
Company profile: Kramp is a European distributor of agricultural parts building an e-commerce and supply chain platform for farmers, dealers and ag-tech customers.

## On the resume

- Built a demand forecasting engine for spare parts in B2B e-commerce supply chains, combining LightGBM with Holt-Winters baselines on seasonal sales history to guide warehouse stock planning.
- Evaluated forecasts with rolling backtests against naive baselines, orchestrated training in Kubeflow pipelines on Google Cloud, and served predictions from a Docker endpoint built with Cloud Build.

**Technologies:** Kubeflow, Cloud Build, Docker, Google Cloud, LightGBM, Python, SQL, FastAPI
