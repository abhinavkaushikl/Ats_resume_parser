What you’ll build and own
Design, build, and maintain data pipelines and workflows that support analytics, reporting, and modelling needs.
Analyze large datasets to identify trends, patterns, and actionable insights for the business.
Build, validate, and maintain statistical or machine learning models to solve business problems.
Ensure data quality, consistency, and integrity across sources and pipelines.
Design and maintain data models, schemas, and warehouses that support scalable analytics.
Collaborate with stakeholders to translate business questions into data solutions and clear reporting.
Contribute to data best practices and continuous improvement initiatives across projects.
Participate in requirement reviews and help identify data risks early in the development process.

Core Requirements 
5+ years of experience in Data Analysis, Data Science, or Data Engineering.
Strong experience with SQL and working with relational and/or NoSQL databases.
Hands-on experience with a programming language such as Python, R, or similar.
Experience building or maintaining data pipelines, ETL/ELT processes, or data warehouses.
Familiarity with data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, Looker, or similar.
Ability to translate business questions into clear, data-driven analysis and recommendations.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
Ability to work independently in a remote, fast-paced environment.
