## Job description

Senior ML Research Scientist
Berlin, Germany | Hybrid, 3 days per week onsite

An ambitious AI research organisation is developing safe-by-design, non-agentic AI focused on understanding and predicting the world rather than acting upon it. As a Senior ML Research Scientist, you’ll work with researchers, mathematicians and ML engineers on advanced deep learning, large-scale models, experimentation and evaluation. This is a hands-on research role where you’ll help develop novel ML approaches and shape the technical direction of a genuinely different AI programme.

Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement novel ML approaches for complex research problems.

- Develop and run large-scale experiments and evaluate model behaviour.

- Adapt and fine-tune advanced and foundation models.

- Build rigorous evaluation methodologies and benchmarks.

- Analyse results and identify new research directions.

- Work closely with researchers, mathematicians and ML engineers.

Required Experience & Skills
Essential

- PhD in Computer Science, ML, Mathematics, Statistics or related field.

- Publications at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, CVPR or similar.

- 3-8 years' experience in ML/deep learning research or industry.

- Hands-on experience developing and training ML models.

- Experience with large-scale models, foundation models or LLMs.

- Strong Python and PyTorch, JAX or TensorFlow skills.

- Experience designing experiments and evaluating ML models.

- Experience with distributed or large-scale ML workloads.

- Strong understanding of modern deep learning methodologies.

- Desirable: open-source contributions; AI safety, alignment, interpretability, robustness or model evaluation experience; Foundation model or frontier AI research experience; Probabilistic modelling, Bayesian methods or statistical ML; NLP, scientific ML or advanced model adaptation experience; Experience with distributed training/inference infrastructure.

Why Apply?

- Work on a fundamentally different approach to advanced AI, centred on safe-by-design, non-agentic systems.

- Contribute directly to research direction rather than simply implementing an established roadmap.

- Collaborate with a highly technical team of researchers, mathematicians and ML engineers.

- Tackle challenging problems across deep learning, foundation models and AI safety.

- Make a meaningful contribution to the development of safer AI and scientific discovery.
