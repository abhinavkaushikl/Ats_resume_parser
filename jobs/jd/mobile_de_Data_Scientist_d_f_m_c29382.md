About the Role

We are seeking a passionate Data Scientist (d/f/m) to join our central Data Science team based in Berlin! Our team’s mission is to deliver data-driven insights and machine learning (ML) solutions at scale that enhance user experiences and drive value across our platforms, with a particular focus on search, ranking, recommenders and personalization. We offer a flexible working environment with in-person presence expected once/twice a week, or as required for key product appointments. 

Job requirements

As a Data Scientist (d/f/m), you will:

- Drive impactful projects that apply data science and advanced ML models to complex business problems, especially around search, ranking, recommendation, and personalization.

- Explore and integrate Generative AI solutions into product features, including LLM-based search enhancements, conversational interfaces, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, and AI-generated content - evaluating their impact on user experience and platform value.

- Own end-to-end data science initiatives: from ideation and prototyping, to production deployment, performance monitoring, and refining for scalability and efficiency.

- Analyze large and complex datasets to uncover actionable insights that inform business strategies and drive user-centric solutions.

- Build and validate proof-of-concepts for innovative features, clearly presenting results and value to diverse stakeholders.

- Act as a bridge between technical and non-technical teams, communicating insights, methodologies, and outcomes effectively.

- Collaborate closely with product managers, UX designers, engineers, and other data scientists to align on search and platform goals and drive cross-functional product development.

- Work closely with data scientists, promoting best practices and continuous team growth.

- Stay current with the latest advancements in data science, integrating new tools, frameworks, or techniques to push the team’s capabilities forward.

Job responsibilities

What You Bring

- Relevant MSc or Ph.D. in statistics, computer science, economics, or a related field.

- 2+ years of hands-on experience in designing, training, and deploying machine learning models, ideally within marketplaces or technology-driven environments.

- Hands-on experience with Generative AI technologies, including LLMs (e.g., OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, LLaMA), vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate, BigQuery Vector Search), and RAG architectures.

- Advanced programming skills in Python (preferred), and proficiency working with SQL and/or NoSQL databases.

- Strong background in machine learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn) and experience with MLOps best practices.

- Proven track record with cloud platforms, especially GCP or AWS, including deploying and managing ML solutions in production environments.

- Confidence in translating complex business needs into data-driven strategies and communicating findings to technical and non-technical audiences.

- Enthusiasm for agile software development (Kanban/Scrum), product discovery, and iterative, continuous deployment.

- Excellent English language communication skills, both written and verbal.

- Passion for innovation, continuous learning, and making a real impact on how millions interact with our platform.
