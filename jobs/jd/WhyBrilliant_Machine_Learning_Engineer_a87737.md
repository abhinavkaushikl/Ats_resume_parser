The Role

Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer

Location: Berlin, Germany

Why this role matters

Traditional factory planning tools are rigid, slow, and unable to adapt to real-time shopfloor disruptions. By building a high-fidelity digital twin for reinforcement learning simulations, we enable manufacturing plants to optimize resources on the fly, transforming raw physical complexity into automated, intelligent decisions.

What You'll Do

You will own the end-to-end translation of physical manufacturing requirements into our reinforcement learning simulation framework.

Who This Is a Fit For

- Python Expert: 5+ years of experience writing robust, scalable, and highly testable Python code.

- Shopfloor Context: 2+ years working in factory operations (as an engineer/planner) or building ERP/MES systems.

- Manufacturing Domain: Strong grasp of manufacturing processes, including discrete, line, and engineer-to-order setups.

- Applied ML: Basic understanding of Reinforcement Learning algorithms and hands-on experience with PyTorch, Optuna, or MLflow.

- Engineering Rigor: Experienced with CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions, unit testing, and integration testing practices.

Team members have ownership from day one, with fast feedback loops and highly visible results, collaborating alongside highly skilled ML specialists and experienced software engineers. Work-life balance is supported through flexible working hours and a remote-first setup, with options to work from their co-working space in Berlin Mitte.
