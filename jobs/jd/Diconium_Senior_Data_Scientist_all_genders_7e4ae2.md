What You Can Expect

- Development and implementation of machine learning algorithms and models to address our clients’ business challenges.

- Extraction of valuable insights from large and unstructured data sources.

- Creation and leadership in the development of AI/ML-driven solutions for predictive analytics, recommendation systems, and process automation.

- Collaboration with cross-functional teams to understand our clients’ business objectives and translate them into effective ML/AI solutions.

- Evaluation of model performance and continuous improvement of accuracy and efficiency through iterative optimization.

- Communication of findings and insights clearly and effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- Assumption of functional leadership responsibilities and support of other data scientists in their professional development.

What You Bring Along

Must-haves

- Bachelor’s degree or higher in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or a related quantitative field.

- 5+ years of relevant experience in data science with proven hands-on experience in applied machine learning, AI and statistical modeling.

- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, including experience with relevant libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, scikit-learn, NLTK), as well as a good command of SQL.

- Great understanding of statistical methods and machine learning algorithms.

- Experience leading projects or taking on functional leadership responsibilities.

- Experience communicating with and managing stakeholders, ideally in the IT services industry or a consulting environment.

- Ability to work both independently and collaboratively in fast-paced environments, with excellent communication skills and the ability to clearly present complex technical concepts.

- Full professional proficiency in German and English (C1 level minimum)

Nice-to-haves

- Experience with big data technologies and cloud platforms. 

- Experience with data processing in Spark and Databricks, or similar technologies 

- Experience with data visualization, dashboarding tools or simple frontends for visualization (Streamlit, Dash, Tableau, PowerBI). 

- Knowledge of deep learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, Keras). 

- Previous experience with ML operations and deploying ML/AI models into production environments. 

- Experience with generative AI and agentic systems.
