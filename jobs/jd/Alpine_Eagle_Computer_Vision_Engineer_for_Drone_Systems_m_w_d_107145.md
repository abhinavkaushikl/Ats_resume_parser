As a Computer Vision Engineer, you will be instrumental in advancing our drone technology. You will develop and optimize algorithms, train machine learning models, and collaborate with a multidisciplinary team to deliver next-generation drone solutions.

🎯 How you will make an impact:

- Collaborate with ML Engineers: Work closely with machine learning engineers to refine and optimize models specifically for aerial scenarios.

- Integration with Hardware: Partner with the hardware team to ensure seamless integration of computer vision models with drone systems.

- Stay Updated: Keep abreast of the latest advancements in computer vision technologies and research, with a special focus on aerial applications.

- Code Reviews and Documentation: Participate in code reviews, maintain thorough documentation, and contribute to team discussions.

- Proven experience with deep learning frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, or equivalent. Experience with CNN, Object Detectors and Tracking.

- Strong proficiency in Python and C++.
