Forward Deployed Engineer – Agentic AI / GenAI

As demand for complex AI transformation programmes continues to grow, they are looking for Forward Deployed Engineers to take ownership of high-impact GenAI and ML implementations across multiple enterprise clients.

The Opportunity
This role sits directly at the intersection of AI engineering, consulting, and delivery leadership.
You will work closely with enterprise customers to design, implement, and operationalise Agentic AI and GenAI solutions, acting as both a senior technical leader and trusted client-facing partner.
The environment is fast-moving, technically ambitious, and highly delivery focused.
You’ll join a technically strong team with significant exposure to executive stakeholders and multi-project ownership.

- Lead the end-to-end delivery of complex GenAI, Agentic AI, and ML transformation programmes across enterprise environments

- Act as the bridge between engineering teams, leadership stakeholders, and business users

- Own technical delivery across multiple concurrent projects with high visibility and commercial impact

- Guide clients on AI adoption, operationalisation, and integration into existing workflows and systems

- Collaborate with internal engineering and consulting teams to ensure successful deployment and measurable outcomes

- Support technical decision-making around AI architecture, tooling, workflows, and scalability

Ideal Background

- Strong experience delivering AI / GenAI / Machine Learning projects in production environments

- Proven track record leading complex customer-facing implementations from discovery through deployment

- Experience working with Agentic AI workflows, LLM-based systems, or modern AI orchestration tooling
