Senior Data Scientist

Shape the Future of AI in Large-Scale Industrial Operations

Are you a Senior Data Scientist who enjoys turning complex operational challenges into practical AI solutions?

What You'll Be Doing

As a Senior Data Scientist, you will work closely with Data Engineers, Data Architects, BI specialists, and business stakeholders to design and deliver production-ready machine learning and AI solutions.
Your responsibilities will include:

- Developing and deploying machine learning models using Python

- Building end-to-end AI solutions from data preparation to production deployment

- Designing predictive analytics and time-series forecasting models

- Creating anomaly detection and operational optimization solutions

- Developing computer vision and document intelligence applications

- Working with LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Generative AI use cases

- Applying MLOps best practices to ensure scalable and reliable deployments

- Translating business requirements into technical solutions

- Communicating findings and recommendations to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

- Collaborating within an Azure-based cloud environment using modern DevOps practices

- 5+ years of experience as a Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, or similar role

- Strong Python development skills

- Experience delivering machine learning solutions in production environments

- Solid understanding of statistics, machine learning, and data modelling

- Experience with Microsoft Azure, ideally Azure ML or related AI services

- Knowledge of Generative AI technologies, including:

- Experience working with Git, CI/CD pipelines, and MLOps practices
