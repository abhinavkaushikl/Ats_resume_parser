Job Summary

ASM Tech Ltd is seeking a **Senior Software Developer with 4 years of professional software development experience** to design, develop, test, maintain and enhance software applications and technology solutions.

The role requires strong hands-on experience in **Java, Python, JavaScript, SQL, REST APIs, database development and workflow automation**, together with the ability to take ownership of software development activities from requirements analysis through design, implementation, testing and deployment.

The successful candidate will work on web applications, backend services, automation solutions, internal tools and AI-enabled technology initiatives.

* Design, develop and maintain AI-enabled software applications and automation solutions.
* Develop using **Java, Python, JavaScript, SQL and REST APIs**.

* Analyse requirements and deliver scalable, reliable software solutions.
* Perform testing, debugging, optimisation and release validation.
* Identify opportunities to automate processes and improve efficiency.
* Collaborate with technical and business teams and maintain technical documentation.
* Support continuous improvement of software quality, security and development practices.
