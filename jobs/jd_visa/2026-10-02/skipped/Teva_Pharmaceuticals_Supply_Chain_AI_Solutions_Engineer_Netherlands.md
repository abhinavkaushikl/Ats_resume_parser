## Job Description

## **We Are Teva**

We’re Teva, a leading innovative biopharmaceutical company, enabled by a world-class generics business. Whether it’s innovating in the fields of neuroscience and immunology or delivering high-quality medicine worldwide, we’re dedicated to addressing patients’ needs now and in the future. Here, you will be part of a high-performing, inclusive culture that values fresh thinking and collaboration. You'll have the room to grow, the flexibility to balance life with work, and the opportunity to better health worldwide, together.

## **Our Team, Your Impact**

This role is expected to work very closely with Supply Chain Analytics, Planning teams, and IT to accelerate the operationalization of advanced analytical, AI-driven, and agent-based capabilities across end-to-end Supply Chain processes.

## **How You’ll Spend Your Day**

* Develop and implement AI-enabled analytical and agent-based solutions supporting Supply Chain decision-making
* Design and operationalize AI agents capable of monitoring planning integrity, KPI deviations, and operational anomalies
* Support optimization and explainability initiatives around IBP RCCP Optimizer outputs
* Build AI-assisted analytics, scenario simulation, and recommendation capabilities
* Develop operational KPI monitoring, anomaly-detection, and predictive alerting capabilities
* Support rapid root-cause analysis and decision-support automation
* Develop AI-supported master data integrity and consistency monitoring frameworks
* Collaborate with Planning, Manufacturing, Analytics, and IT teams to translate operational needs into scalable solutions
* Support integration of external and internal data sources into analytical and AI-agent models
* Contribute to the industrialization of AI-enabled Supply Chain capabilities together with IT teams

## **Your Skills and Experience**

* Strong hands-on experience in AI/ML-enabled analytics environments
* Experience developing AI-driven, agent-based, or analytical solutions in Supply Chain or Operations
* Strong Python / data analytics capabilities
* Experience with optimization or advanced planning environments
* Exposure to SAP IBP / supply planning processes is highly desirable
* Experience with anomaly detection, monitoring, or decision-support environments
* Ability to operate in highly cross-functional business environments

## **Also Good to Have**

* Pharma or regulated industry experience
* Exposure to AI agents, and decision intelligence environments
* Experience with scenario planning or optimization frameworks
* Exposure to master data governance or planning data integrity initiatives
