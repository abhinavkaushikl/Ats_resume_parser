## Job description

**Who You'll Work With**Driving lasting impact and building long-term capabilities with our clients is not easy work. You are the kind of person who thrives in a high performance/high reward culture - doing hard things, picking yourself up when you stumble, and having the resilience to try another way forward.

In return for your drive, determination, and curiosity, we'll provide the resources, mentorship, and opportunities you need to become a stronger leader faster than you ever thought possible. Your colleagues—at all levels—will invest deeply in your development, just as much as they invest in delivering exceptional results for clients. Every day, you'll receive apprenticeship, coaching, and exposure that will accelerate your growth in ways you won’t find anywhere else.

When you join us, you will have:


* Continuous learning: Our learning and apprenticeship culture, backed by structured programs, is all about helping you grow while creating an environment where feedback is clear, actionable, and focused on your development. The real magic happens when you take the input from others to heart and embrace the fast-paced learning experience, owning your journey.
* A voice that matters: From day one, we value your ideas and contributions. You’ll make a tangible impact by offering innovative ideas and practical solutions, all while upholding our unwavering commitment to ethics and integrity. We not only encourage diverse perspectives, but they are critical in driving us toward the best possible outcomes.
* Global community: With colleagues across 65+ countries and over 100 different nationalities, our firm’s diversity fuels creativity and helps us come up with the best solutions for our clients. Plus, you’ll have the opportunity to learn from exceptional colleagues with diverse backgrounds and experiences.
* World-class benefits: On top of a competitive salary (based on your location, experience, and skills), we provide a comprehensive benefits package to enable holistic well-being for you and your family.

**Your Impact**You will join a consulting team and take ownership of a specific project workstream.

You will work closely with our clients to create scalable value through technology and Artificial Intelligence (AI). Our clients include some of the world’s most influential organizations, ranging from large global corporations to innovative start-ups.

You will tackle significant challenges while we accelerate your professional growth. You will design solutions for today's most critical strategic challenges, driving long-term transformations.

You will work side-by-side with our clients on-site, helping them maximize the potential of data and AI to solve critical business problems, refine their strategies, and execute complex transformations.

You will develop AI-driven solutions, modernize technology infrastructures, optimize and automate processes, drive digital growth, and empower teams to effectively adopt AI and digital technologies.

Together with your clients and McKinsey colleagues, you will combine research, data analysis, interviews, and ideation sessions to generate valuable insights and design innovative solutions.

By equipping our clients with the right tools and capabilities, you will ensure our projects continue to drive innovation and continuous improvement long after completion, creating a lasting impact.

You will contribute to McKinsey's purpose of making a meaningful difference across industries and society as a whole.
