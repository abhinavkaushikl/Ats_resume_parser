**Principal Machine Learning Scientist (all genders)**

**YOUR TASKS AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

* You lead the development, evaluation, and application of machine learning algorithms and workflows for the characterization and design of biomolecules to accelerate drug discovery
* You identify opportunities for accelerating ongoing drug discovery projects with internal and external AI capabilities
* You communicate, educate, and engage with a broad set of stakeholders (chemists, biologists, computational/data scientists, R&D leadership, consortia partners, and the broader scientific community) on the state of technology and the progress of key internal initiatives
* You keep up to date with the latest advances in computational modeling of biomolecular structure, physics, interactions, and function

**WHO YOU ARE**

* You hold a PhD degree in computational chemistry/biology, chem/bioinformatics, chemical/biological/molecular engineering, or a related field at the intersection of life sciences and computer sciences
* You have multiple years of relevant post-PhD experience, including professional experience in industry
* You have expertise with state-of-the-art machine learning methods to model biomolecules, for example sequence-to-function models, protein language models, co-folding, inverse folding or steerable generative methods
* You have experience with ML-based workflows towards characterization of biomolecular interactions, assessment of druggability, candidate screening, or design and optimization of therapeutics and delivery systems.
* You are experienced in handling, processing, integrating and analyzing large datasets related to drug development research, including sequence, omics, biochemical, biophysical, and structural biology data
* Expertise in modern bioinformatics tools (e.g., mmseqs, foldseek) is part of your profile
* You have strong Python programming skills including the scientific stack (e.g. pytorch, pandas, scikit-learn), and experience in collaborative software engineering according to good practices
* You have a strong commitment to scientific rigor, a proven track record of scientific achievement, excellent analytical thinking skills, and a high level of self-motivation
* Excellent written and verbal communication skill in English round off your profile
