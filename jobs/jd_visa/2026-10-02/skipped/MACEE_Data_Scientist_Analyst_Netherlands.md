Job description

As a Data Scientist, you will play a critical role in supporting and optimizing Rabobank's Transaction Monitoring capabilities. Through advanced analytics, dashboard development, and stakeholder collaboration, you will help colleagues make data-driven decisions that increase the effectiveness of detecting potential money laundering and terrorist financing activities.

What You Will Do

* Perform end-to-end analytical projects, from business intake and requirements gathering to coding, analysis, visualization, and presentation of results.
* Design, build, and maintain impactful Power BI dashboards that support Transaction Monitoring optimization and decision-making.
* Develop scalable and maintainable analytical solutions using Python.
* Estimate and assess the impact of proposed changes to Transaction Monitoring rules and parameters
* Translate complex business questions into analytical approaches and actionable recommendations.
* Act as a trusted partner for stakeholders, helping them formulate the right questions and uncover opportunities through data
* Drive continuous improvements and proactively identify areas where analytics can create measurable business value
* Collaborate closely with data professionals, business experts, and other stakeholders across multidisciplinary teams.

Examples of Assignments

* Develop a Transaction Monitoring rule tuning dashboard to support optimization of monitoring parameters
* Build management dashboards that provide deeper insight into the effectiveness of the Transaction Monitoring system.
* Analyze customer transaction behavior to identify patterns and answer business-critical questions
* Quantify the expected impact of model and rule adjustments on operational efficiency and detection effectiveness

Requirements

Required Qualification

* Master's degree in a quantitative or technical field
* Minimum 3 years of relevant experience as a Data Scientist, Data Analyst, or similar analytical role
* Strong Power BI expertise, including advanced dashboard development, data visualization, and storytelling with data
* Solid Python programming skills, including:
* Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)
* Software engineering best practice
* Coding standards and code reviews
* Writing maintainable and scalable code
* Proven ability to independently lead initiatives and drive results.
* Strong communication skills with the ability to translate business needs into analytical solutions
* Structured, proactive, and highly organized working style
* Strong stakeholder management and collaboration skills
* Business-focused mindset with a strong sense of ownership.
* Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English.

Preferred Profile

* Experience working in Financial Economic Crime (FEC), AML, Transaction Monitoring, Risk, or Compliance environment
* Ability to balance technical depth with business impact.
* Comfortable operating in a dynamic, fast-paced environment
* Motivated by delivering measurable improvements rather than building analytics solutions for their own sake.
