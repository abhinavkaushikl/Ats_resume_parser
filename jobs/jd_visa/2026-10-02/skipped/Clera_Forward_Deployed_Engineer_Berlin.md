## Job description

### About the Role

This is a hands-on, customer-facing engineering role at a pre-seed AI fintech startup building automation tools for private-market operations. You will own technical delivery end-to-end, from early discovery with asset servicers and asset managers through production launch of AI-driven fund accounting workflows. Your work directly shapes both customer outcomes and the core product roadmap.

### What You'll Do

* Lead the full project lifecycle for customer deployments, from initial discovery through production go-live.
* Work directly with customer operations teams to map existing workflows and identify adoption barriers.
* Convert customer processes into AI-driven, automated workflows using the platform.
* Build customer-tailored prototypes and iterate quickly based on real feedback and shifting scope.
* Feed deployment insights back into product development planning to improve the platform.
* Develop standardized approaches and reusable components from patterns observed across deployments.

### What We're Looking For

* 1 to 6 years of experience shipping production software, with strong TypeScript and Node.js proficiency (NestJS, Postgres, Prisma, React).
* Hands-on experience working directly with customers to scope, prototype, and iterate on technical deliverables, such as forward-deployed or solutions engineering roles.
* Genuine empathy for operational workflows and the ability to translate business processes into technical solutions.
* Comfort owning deliverables independently across the full technical lifecycle.
* Background in private markets, fund accounting, or financial operations is a strong plus.

### Compensation & Benefits

Salary range: **70,000 to 110,000 EUR annually**. Visa sponsorship is available.

### Location

On-site in **Berlin, Germany**. Candidates must be able to work regularly from the Berlin office.
