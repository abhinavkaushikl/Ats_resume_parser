## Job description

Our Applied AI Engineering team designs and builds solutions across the spectrum — from data processing, through AI and ML workflows, to agentic AI — often in complex, regulated environments such as financial services. We work at the edge of delivery: embedded with the business, moving fast, and proving what's worth building. We're now looking for a Forward-Deployed Data & AI Engineer to sit at that edge — turning ambiguous business problems into working solutions, fast, from the raw data all the way to the AI.

As a **Forward-Deployed Data & AI Engineer** within EY's Applied AI Engineering team, you will be embedded directly with our clients' business teams — helping to find high-value problems and turning them into working solutions in weeks rather than months.

You work at the **early discovery and prototyping as well as productization stage of delivery**: hands-on, agile, and close to the business. This is a full-spectrum build role — you'll wrangle messy, real-world data into lightweight pipelines and data products, and then build the AI/ML workflows, GenAI and agentic solutions on top of them. Together with your Tech Lead and the wider pod, you'll prove (or disprove) value quickly, and help shape the requirements a validated idea needs before it's moved to production to scale.

This is a hands-on engineering role for a versatile builder who is energised by ambiguity, comfortable across both the data and the AI, enjoys working directly with business users, and would rather ship a rough working demo than a polished slide.

**EY.AI** – in this role, you will work in an environment where AI agents are an integral part of the team and daily project delivery. You will leverage role-specific AI solutions that help analyse data, generate recommendations, and design innovative solutions, enabling you to move faster from insight to action and create value for clients.

**Your scope of duties**

* Work embedded with client business teams to understand problems in context and turn them into working solutions — end to end, from data to AI.
* Rapidly profile and wrangle source data, and stand up lightweight pipelines and integrations to get prototypes working — ingesting, cleaning, transforming and joining messy, real-world data.
* Design, build and demonstrate AI/ML workflows, GenAI and agentic-AI solutions on top of that data, using cloud-native services.
* Iterate fast on feedback — build, demo, learn, repeat — to prove or kill ideas quickly.
* Help distil clear data and solution requirements, source mappings and de-risked designs.
* Build responsibly from the start — mindful of data quality, lineage, privacy, guardrails and compliance (e.g. EU AI Act, GDPR, data residency).
* Collaborate closely with your Tech Lead, the architects and business stakeholders.
* Contribute to the team's reusable accelerators, connectors, assets and engineering practices.

**Skills And Attributes For Success**

* Comfort with ambiguity and messy data — you can make progress without clean sources or a complete specification.
* Full-spectrum, hands-on craft — SQL and Python, data pipelines, ML workflows, and LLM / agent frameworks — across both the data and the AI.
* Speed and a demo-first mindset — you build the simplest thing that proves the point, and you'd rather show than tell.
* Business and domain awareness — you keep the user's real problem in focus and understand what the data means, not just its shape.
* Collaboration and curiosity — you learn fast and work well in a small, fast-moving team.

**Our Requirements**

* A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field, and
* For senior position approximately 3+ years of hands-on data / AI / software engineering experience (or a master's degree and approximately 2 years).
* Strong Python and SQL, with experience both building data pipelines (ETL / ELT) and building AI/ML or GenAI solutions.
* Experience with LLM / agent frameworks (e.g. Semantic Kernel, Azure AI Agent Service, LangChain, LangGraph, Bedrock Agents) and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) patterns.
* The ability to work with data end to end — profiling, wrangling, integrating and modelling messy, real-world data.
* Hands-on cloud experience — ideally Microsoft Azure (e.g. Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Foundry / Azure Machine Learning, Azure Functions, Azure Data Factory, Microsoft Fabric / Synapse), and/or AWS (Bedrock, SageMaker, Lambda, Glue) or Google Cloud (Vertex AI, BigQuery, Dataflow).
* Good communication skills and comfort working directly with non-technical stakeholders.

**Ideally, you'll also have**

* Financial-services or other regulated-industry experience, and awareness of relevant controls (AML/KYC, EU AI Act, GDPR, data residency).
* Experience preparing data for AI/ML or GenAI — features, embeddings and retrieval corpora.
* Rapid-prototyping, hackathon or startup experience.
* Exposure to MLOps / DataOps / LLMOps and to preparing prototypes for production handoff.
* Familiarity with modern data tooling (e.g. dbt, Spark, Kafka).
* Cloud certifications (e.g. Azure, AWS or Google Cloud).
* A consulting or product background.
