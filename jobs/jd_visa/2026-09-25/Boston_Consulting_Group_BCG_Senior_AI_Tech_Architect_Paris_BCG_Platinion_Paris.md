What You'll Do

As a (Senior) AI Architect, you will partner with client leadership teams to identify, design, and deliver high-impact AI-driven solutions with strong business relevance. You will help organizations translate their strategic ambitions into tangible outcomes—ranging from core system modernization in the banking industry to cutting-edge engineering use cases powered by Generative AI.


* Working closely with BCG and BCG Platinion teams, you will shape and implement digital initiatives that combine advanced technology, software craftsmanship, and business insight. You will design and deliver end-to-end AI solutions, covering problem framing, data strategy, model integration, and scalable deployment while ensuring measurable value creation.
* You will take ownership of key workstreams, guide client technology and business teams, and ensure delivery quality through agile and modern software engineering practices. You will actively contribute to the diffusion of GenAI capabilities across industries, from experimentation to industrialization.
* You will contribute to BCG Platinion’s digital and technology agenda by:

+ Developing innovative AI use cases that accelerate client transformation and competitiveness.
+ Supporting business development through thought leadership, proposals, and client relationship management.
+ Contributing to internal initiatives that strengthen BCG Platinion’s expertise in GenAI and enhance BCG’s positioning as a leader in AI-enabled transformation.

* You will also benefit from structured training programs, mentorship, and continuous learning opportunities, enabling you to grow as both a technologist and a consultant combining deep technical understanding with strategic business impact.
