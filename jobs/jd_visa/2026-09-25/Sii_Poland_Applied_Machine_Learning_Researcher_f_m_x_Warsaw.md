## Job description

For our client in the banking industry, we are seeking an Applied Machine Learning Researcher who will join a high-impact Applied AI research team.

You will work on impactful initiatives that combine research and practical implementation, helping solve complex and unique challenges present within the financial domain.

 ****Your tasks****

* Working closely with cross-functional teams to improve and scale production machine learning systems and applications

* Openness to hybrid work from the Warsaw office
