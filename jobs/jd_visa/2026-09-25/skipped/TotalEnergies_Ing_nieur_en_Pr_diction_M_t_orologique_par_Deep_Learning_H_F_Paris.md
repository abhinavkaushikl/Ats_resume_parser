## Job description

****Contexte et environnement****TotalEnergies has launched an ambitious Integrated Power Modeling initiative designed to secure a leading position in nextgeneration energy systems and electricity markets.

As renewable energy sources are increasingly integrated into power grids andindustries, transportation, and buildings undergo large-scale electrification, the energy landscape is evolving at an unprecedentedpace.

In this context, advanced modeling and forecasting solutions to support supply-demand balance management have become critical enablers of strategic decision-making and long-term competitiveness.

 ****Activités****

* Support short and medium-term commercial ambitions in weather forecasting
* Collect and acquire the most relevant datasets both open-source and proprietary tailored to specific geographical areas
* Design and develop robust internal workflows based on deep learning solutions
* Continuously integrate and evaluate the latest models from major tech companies, startups, and academia
* Create educational content adapted for a general audience
* Produce scientific documentation to explain internal developments and contribute to scientific conferences
* Provide support for the adoption and use of internal forecasting tools within the organization
* Conduct both industrial and academic monitoring to stay at the forefront of weather forecasting technologies
* Identify and collaborate with stakeholders at all levels: academia, industry, business units, and programs
* Contribute to team skill development through training, workshops, and knowledge-sharing initiatives

 ****Profil du candidat****

* Master's or PhD in deep learning with a specialization in meteorological modeling.
* Hands-on knowledge of deep learning-based weather forecasting models such as GraphCast, Aurora, and FourCastNet.
* Hands-on knowledge of the most relevant input data for weather forecasting: meteorological agencies, satellite imagery, and local observations.
* Practical knowledge of the prerequisites for industrializing deep learning.
* Proficiency in computing infrastructures, both cloud and HPC including Slurm, as well as storage architectures.
* More than 6 years of professional experience.
* English and French required.
