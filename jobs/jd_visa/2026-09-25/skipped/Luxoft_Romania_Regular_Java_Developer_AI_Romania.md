🚀 We are hiring: Regular Java Developer + AI (Hybrid - Bucharest) | Luxoft

* **3+ years** of software engineering experience.

* **Generative AI Exposure:** Understanding or hands-on experience with LLMs, RAG architectures, **Spring AI**, or Python AI frameworks (**LangChain, LlamaIndex**).
* **Messaging & Cloud:** Experience with streaming platforms (**Apache Kafka**, IBM MQ) and cloud/container techs (**GCP**, Docker, Kubernetes, OpenShift).
* **DevOps & Testing:** Familiarity with TDD/BDD practices and tools like Maven, Gradle, Git, and Jenkins/TeamCity.

* Work on high-impact systems protecting financial compliance on a global scale.
