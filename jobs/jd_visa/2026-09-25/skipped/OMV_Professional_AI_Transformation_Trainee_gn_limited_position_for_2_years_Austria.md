## Job description

**Your tasks**

* Identify AI opportunities and develop practical business solutions that improve everyday work.
* Collaborate directly with the IT and business teams to transform ideas into impactful AI use cases.
* Act as AI Transformation Ambassador and coach colleagues on all AI related topics within the business unit
* Facilitate workshops to demonstrate AI opportunities, gather business requirements and trigger re-thinking of existing workflows
* Create AI applications using Generative AI, Agentic, Machine Learning and digital automation technologies.
* Support the adoption of AI capabilities out of enterprise platforms and digital products.
* Monitor, validate and continuously improve AI solutions based on user feedback.
* Collaborate with data scientists, engineers and digital experts in agile product teams to scale AI services
* Contribute to AI communities by sharing best practices, practical examples, latest technology developments and new ideas.
* Promote responsible AI practices with a focus on security, compliance and ethical use and support AI adoption across OMV and shape the future of work.

**Your profile**

* You are pursuing or have just graduated with a Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Business Analytics, Business Administration, Engineering, Information Technology or a related field.
* Professional experience in AI, data analytics, software development or digital technologies is an advantage.
* First practical experience with Generative AI, Agentic solutions, machine learning, automation tools or data analytics and ability to apply AI & Data services within your field of expertise
* Knowledge of Python, Cloud platforms, enterprise software (SAP, ServiceNow, Salesforce, …) or latest AI technologies & platforms is an advantage.
* Strong analytical thinking, and curiosity to explore emerging technologies as well as to work with various stakeholders
* Fluent English skills with the ability to explain technical concepts to diverse audiences.
